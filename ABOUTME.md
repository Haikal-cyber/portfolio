Project 8 – Uber Ride Cancellation & Cab Availability Analysis
About the Project:
This project analyzes Uber ride data from an UpGrad case study, focusing on the challenges of ride cancellations and cab unavailability for users traveling between the airport and the city.
The goal was to uncover key patterns and insights behind these issues, and provide data-driven recommendations to enhance service reliability and reduce business losses.

What i Did:

* Conducted Data Cleaning & Preprocessing to ensure the dataset was ready for analysis
* Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to identify trends and anomalies
* Built visual dashboards to compare ride statuses (Cancelled, No Cars Available, Trip Completed) across pickup locations (Airport vs City)
* Delivered business insights and recommendations to improve cab allocation and minimize cancellations

Key Insights:
* A large number of users at the airport experienced "No Cars Available" (over 50%)
* In contrast, city users had a significantly higher cancellation rate (over 30%)
* The imbalance between demand and supply across locations led to suboptimal service
